About Us

A safe home where 40+ children belong.

40+ Orphaned & Vulnerable Children Currently Under Care

Faith-based • Community-led • Child-focused

Bethel Fellowship Centre is a Kenyan Community-Based Organisation (CBO) headquartered in Oyugis, Homa Bay County, currently providing a loving home to 40+ orphaned and vulnerable children. We exist to rescue children from neglect and poverty, and to raise them in a stable family environment.

What began in 2020 as a small fellowship responding to children sleeping hungry and out of school has grown into a registered home for 40+ children offering residential care, education support, daily feeding, spiritual nurture, psychosocial counseling, and community outreach to guardians and widows.

We are not an institution that warehouses children — we are a family. We keep siblings together, celebrate birthdays, go to church together, do homework together, and work toward family reunification or responsible transition whenever safe and possible.

Vision

A Kenya where every orphaned child grows up safe, educated, loved, and able to fulfill God's purpose.

Mission

To rescue, raise and restore vulnerable children through holistic residential care, education, nutrition, spiritual nurture and community strengthening.

Our Story

From a small room in Oyugis to a registered home of hope.

2020

Founder Victor Odhiambo opens his home to 3 children left without caregivers during COVID-19 lockdowns. Neighbors bring food, a local church provides mats. The seed for today's 40+ children home.

2021–23

Word spreads. Children increase from 3 to 18, then beyond. Volunteer teachers start evening classes. Feeding program launched to reach guardians in the village.

2024

Officially registered as CBO Reg No. 29.01.18/CBO/037/2024. Home now cares for 40+ children. Governance structures, child protection policy and financial accountability adopted.

Holistic care for body, mind and spirit.

We don't run projects — we run a home. Each pillar is designed to give children stability today and skills for tomorrow.

01

Residential Care

Safe dorms, separate for boys/girls, matron care, clean water, bedding, daily routines. Family-style supervision and child protection protocols.

  • 24/7 caregiver presence
  • Case files & health records
  • Family tracing & reunification where safe
02

Education

School fees, uniforms, books, tutoring. Every child enrolled in local primary/secondary. Mentorship for KCPE/KCSE.

03

Feeding & Nutrition

Three balanced meals + porridge. Kitchen garden training. Support for guardians during holidays.

04

Spiritual Nurture & Psychosocial

Daily devotions, counseling, trauma-informed care, play therapy, church community.

05

Community Outreach

Home visits, widow support, jiggers treatment campaigns, school advocacy and child rights awareness in Oyugis.

Led locally, accountable globally.
